**Note, 2026-10-31 (U6 verification):** two placements depart from a record and are kept, and three inverted imports gain an owner.

- Row 3 departs from `docs/engine-platform-architecture.md` (its `entrypoints/` row lists "CLI commands"): `cli.ts` stays in `composition/` until its command parsing is split from its wiring, a code change for step 6; then the parsing goes to `entrypoints/`.
- Row 9 departs from the inventory's row for `shutdown.ts` (module 29, `composition`): `create-app.ts` in `entrypoints/` imports its `Lifecycle`, and placing it in `composition/` would make an entry point import the composition root.
- `runner.ts` sits in `entrypoints/` as the queue's consumer, as the inventory places it, but it holds more than the `entrypoints/` row allows (about 40 branches, imports of persistence, the config loader and engine policies). Step 6 takes those decisions out or reclassifies it; it is listed with the couplings of row 16.
- Row 16's couplings also include `telemetry/metrics.ts` importing `composition/config-loader.ts` and `persistence/types.ts` importing `entrypoints/contract/commands.ts`; both are owned by step 6 with the others (the first becomes an injected app list, the second a type the contract and persistence share from the engine or the contract alone).

**Note, 2026-10-31 (T13 U8, platform step 6):** the owners named above carried out, at 89dbd747 and after.

- `cli.ts` (row 3): its argument parsing is `src/entrypoints/cli-args.ts`; `src/composition/cli.ts` keeps the wiring and prints a refusal.
- `runner.ts`: its run decisions left for the engine (the campaign freeze is `run-orchestration/domain/run-freeze.ts`; how a cancelled or interrupted run ends is `run-orchestration/domain/unfinished-run.ts`), and it takes the app loader from the process roots (`RunnerDeps.loadApp`, required). It stays in `entrypoints/` as the queue's consumer.
- `telemetry/metrics.ts` takes the metered apps (name and repository) as an injected list; `persistence/types.ts` reads the activity kinds (`shared-kernel/activity-kind.ts`) and the change-entry record (`shared-kernel/change-set.ts`) from the kernel, and the run store reads the campaign identity from run-orchestration.
- Row 16's couplings into `composition/` and out of `settings/` are gone and held by rules (`platform-composition-is-the-root`, `platform-settings-reads-no-other-folder`); the API declares its app-admin and evaluation ports, the app-config load errors are app-catalog's, and the telemetry config, the backend-health addresses, the sandbox switches and the tools port are read in `settings/`.
- Row 7 stays open: 64 production imports between `entrypoints/`, `persistence/`, `telemetry/` and `agent-runtime/` remain, 29 of them the loggers and the sanitizer and 18 the stored run shapes and `getHistory`, plus 21 into `settings/` (its accessors and paths); none is ruled. Whether those leaves stay shared or become injected ports is a decision no record settles (`docs/engine-platform-architecture.md` says the four folders do not import each other's internals and leaves `settings/` out of that sentence; ADR-0002 says the six subfolders meet only in `composition/`).

**Note, 2026-10-31 (T13 U7, roots renamed):** row 7 is deferred, under the owner's standing instruction to take the recommended option and record it (ADR-0015, point 2). No run observed a failure that this rule would have prevented. Turning the shared loggers and the sanitizer into injected ports would change where a process that installs no logger writes its lines: the CLI and the scripts reach `appLogger` today and print redacted JSON, and a process that injected nothing would print unredacted console lines. The rule between `entrypoints/`, `persistence/`, `telemetry/` and `agent-runtime/` is therefore not written, and the 64 imports stay as row 7 left them. The roots are now `platform/` and `engine/`; the paths in the rows above are the ones the records cited when they were written.
